# Contributing

Corrections should name the exact v1 paper section and one-based PDF page, explain the discrepancy, and supply a precise primary-source citation. Preserve all four research authors' credit and the distinction between the source paper and this unofficial companion.

Keep conceptual arguments, interpretations of cited studies, speculation, normative claims, and proposed future work separate. Do not add experimental results fields for this paper. If documenting a cited study in more detail, read that study directly and record the exact version, methods, limits, and access route.

Do not silently correct incomplete source citations or substitute a later paper version. Note the source discrepancy and make any verified resolution explicit. Do not add PDFs, full copyrighted text, original tables/figures, signed download links, private identifiers, or personal data.

Update related documentation and structured metadata together. Run `python scripts/validate_repository.py`. Link or metadata tests check repository integrity, not the truth of the paper's arguments.
