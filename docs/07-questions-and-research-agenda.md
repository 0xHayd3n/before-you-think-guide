# 7. Questions and a research agenda

[Guide home](../README.md) · [Previous: paper map](06-paper-map.md)

The activities below are **companion discussion prompts and proposed future work**. They are not experiments or interventions performed by the paper or this repository.

## Check your understanding

1. Why can personalized filtering be both a psychological and an epistemic issue?
2. What additional evidence is needed to move from a useful external tool to a constitutive component of cognition?
3. Why does the paper treat System 0 as a gradual, user-specific relationship rather than a category of devices?
4. Which criticisms of System 3 concern the experimental paradigm, and which concern the concept of surrender?
5. Can a Thinkframes account accommodate personalization while retaining an ecological level of analysis?
6. Why does a persistent AI effect support carryover without automatically proving cognitive colonization?
7. What makes reflective endorsement a normative condition rather than another performance outcome?
8. How can a support work well functionally while still failing a person's reflective aims?
9. Is the native/non-native distinction necessary for the colonization concept?
10. Which rows of Table 2 would actually distinguish theories, and which are compatible with several?

Read [§§2–5.1, pp. 2–24](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.13658v1#page=2) before answering. A good answer should mark where the paper supplies an argument, where it cites external evidence, and where further interpretation begins.

## Turn a theory into a discriminating study

### 1. Specify the unit and exposure

Define the person, task, system, time horizon, and interactions being studied. Record whether assistance is episodic or ambient; whether it adapts to the individual; and what information it filters before an explicit request. A one-session chatbot experiment and months of habitual recommendation use cannot silently stand in for one another.

### 2. Separate candidate mechanisms

Measure answer acceptance, perceived authorship, reliance, skill, carryover, and awareness separately. Include controls for ordinary learning, priming, adviser trust, task difficulty, and differences in information quality. Otherwise a finding can be consistent with System 0 while remaining equally consistent with a simpler account.

### 3. Make theory comparisons explicit

Construct conditions representing external consultation, environmental curation, and personalized integration. Match relevant advice and content where possible. State in advance which patterns each theory predicts and which results would undermine the distinctive System 0 explanation.

A human-adviser condition would address the conjecture in §3 that some apparent surrender reflects ordinary deference. It would not by itself test the full System 0 architecture. [§3, pp. 10–12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.13658v1#page=10)

### 4. Use withdrawal carefully

Following the paper's suggested breakdown strategy, compare performance and experience with support present, absent, and restored. Measure baseline unaided ability and exposure history. Distinguish transient disruption from persistent changes, and compare with withdrawal of familiar non-AI supports. [Footnote 7, p. 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.13658v1#page=15)

### 5. Operationalize the complete proposed profile

Table 3 motivates a joint assessment:

- What evidence supports habitual integration and downstream incorporation?
- Which relevant objectives originate outside the person?
- What does the person know about that direction at the point of use?
- Under what conditions would the person endorse or reject the shaping?

These questions need independent measures. A single survey item or a harm outcome cannot substitute for all four. [Table 3, pp. 21–22](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.13658v1#page=21)

### 6. Elicit endorsement without defining it into existence

Offer meaningful information about selection objectives, alternatives, and consequences. Give participants time to reflect and ways to revise answers. Record disagreements between immediate preference, considered endorsement, and later reflection. A person may reasonably endorse one tradeoff while rejecting another.

### 7. Report uncertainty and scope

A future study should preregister primary outcomes and comparisons where appropriate, report attrition and exclusions, provide uncertainty, and distinguish observed associations from causal effects. Avoid generalizing from one domain to all AI use or converting a non-significant contrast into proof that theories are identical.

## Design and governance questions

The conclusion motivates several practical questions without testing answers: Can users see which goals guide selection? Can they change those goals? Is independent exploration possible? Does a system permit productive friction and dissent? Can institutions inspect personalization without needing to infer its objectives from outputs alone? [§6, pp. 24–26](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.13658v1#page=24)

These questions can inform design reviews. Claims that a particular control preserves agency or reverses colonization would require evidence beyond this conceptual paper.
